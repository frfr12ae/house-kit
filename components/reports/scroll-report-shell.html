    Consolidating three depots into two saves money only if the routes change first

    A six-month look at cost, service and risk, written for the operations committee.

    Prepared for Operations committeeStatus Draft for discussion

    01 · Cost

    Closing the smallest depot removes a fifth of fixed cost

    Rent, utilities and supervision at the smallest depot come to about 19% of the network's fixed cost. The stock it holds can move to the other two sites without new racking.

    • 19%Of fixed cost at one site
    • €410kAnnual saving, gross
    • 14 moPayback on the move

    02 · Service

    Delivery times slip by a day in the north unless routes are redrawn

    The closing depot serves 31 postcode areas. Without new routes, the longest runs grow by 40 minutes each way, which pushes the last drop past the promised window.

    Redrawing routes before the move, not after, keeps on-time delivery above 96%.

    03 · Risk

    Three things could delay the move, and two are in our control

    1. Lease exit date. Fixed by the landlord; not negotiable before March.
    2. Staff transfer. Needs consultation to start by December.
    3. Peak season. A move in November would collide with the busiest weeks.

    04 · Decision

    Approve the route redesign now and decide on the move in January

    The redesign has value whichever way the committee goes, and it removes the main service risk from the decision.

    Ask of the committeeRelease budget for the route study, six weeks, then return with a go or no-go.

    Anticipated questions

    What we expect to be asked

    • The two larger sites carry the specialist cold storage. Closing either would mean rebuilding it elsewhere, which erases the saving for at least five years.

    • Every role has a matching role at one of the two remaining sites. Transfers are offered first; redundancy is only considered for roles with no match.

    • Rent and utilities are contracted figures. Supervision cost is an estimate and the main source of uncertainty, roughly plus or minus €40k.

    • Not cheaply. Once the lease is exited the site is gone, which is why the route study comes first.
