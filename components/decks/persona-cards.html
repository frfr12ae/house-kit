Customers

Three kinds of pet owners want the same thing: less worry, delivered in their own way

  • SK

    Sofie, 29

    First-time dog owner, Copenhagen

    "I just want someone to tell me what is normal and what is not."

    Goals

    Feel confident about daily care and get quick answers.

    Frustrations

    Clinic phone lines close at 16:00, and advice online contradicts itself.

    PrefersApp chat
  • TH

    Thomas, 46

    Two cats and a busy job, Aarhus

    "If it takes more than two taps, I will do it next week."

    Goals

    Keep vaccinations on time without having to remember them.

    Frustrations

    Paper records, missed reminders and surprise bills.

    PrefersEmail and text reminders
  • IM

    Ingrid, 67

    Retired, senior dog, Odense

    "He is family. I will pay for the best, but I want to understand it."

    Goals

    Manage long-term conditions and see costs before they happen.

    Frustrations

    Long travel to clinics and jargon in the invoices.

    PrefersPhone call, then home visit
