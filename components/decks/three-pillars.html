Strategy on a page

Three pillars carry the growth plan: build the network, win e-commerce, run lean

  1. 01

    Build the network

    Two automated hubs lift sorting capacity by 60%.

    Six refreshed depots feed them with shorter runs.

  2. 02

    Win e-commerce

    Next-day delivery reaches 95% of postcodes.

    No single client exceeds 15% of volume.

  3. 03

    Run it lean

    Nightly route optimisation removes idle mileage.

    Cost per parcel falls from EUR 3.10 to EUR 2.60.

TogetherEUR 140m of extra revenue and three points of EBIT margin by 2029.
