Depot network review · Cost

Closing the smallest depot cuts fixed cost by a fifth, provided routes are redrawn first

  • 01

    The saving is real

    Rent, utilities and supervision at the smallest site come to 19% of fixed cost, about €410k a year.

  • 02

    Capacity is not the constraint

    The two remaining depots can absorb the stock without new racking, peaking at 88% of floor space.

  • 03

    Routes are the constraint

    Without a redesign, the longest runs grow by 40 minutes and the last drop misses its window.
