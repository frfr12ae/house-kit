Business model · Halden Cycles

Halden sells a premium commuter e-bike through dealers, with service as the next revenue stream

Key partners

  • Two drive-motor suppliers
  • Cell manufacturers
  • Independent dealers
  • Employer leasing platforms

Key activities

  • Frame and drive design
  • Assembly in our own plant
  • Dealer training

Key resources

  • Battery and motor IP
  • Assembly plant
  • Service data from 90,000 bikes

Value proposition

  • A commuter e-bike built to last ten years
  • 120 km range on one charge
  • Service plan handled for you

Customer relationships

  • Dealer-led advice
  • App with service reminders
  • Owner community

Channels

  • 112 dealers
  • Web shop
  • Leasing portals

Customer segments

  • Urban commuters
  • Employees on bike leasing
  • City delivery fleets
  • Riders over 55

Cost structure, share of cost

  • Components 46%
  • Assembly labour 12%
  • Dealer margin 18%
  • R&D 8%
  • Marketing 5%
  • Other 11%

Revenue streams, share of revenue

  • Bike sales 84%
  • Service plans 9%
  • Battery upgrades 4%
  • Leasing fees 3%
