Workshop, 90 minutes

By the end of today

01

Name your top three risks

Rank what could sink the launch by impact and likelihood, as a group.

Output One ranked risk list
02

Give every risk an owner

Match each risk to a named person and the first check they will run.

Output Owner map
03

Agree the first 30 days

Turn the list into dated actions that fit inside the current sprint plan.

Output 30-day action plan
Tick them off as we go.
