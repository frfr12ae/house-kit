AI ways of working09:45 · Discussion

Where would you not let AI near a client deliverable, and why?

  • Think of the last thing a client was unhappy about.
  • What would you need to see before you trusted the output?
  • Who signs off, and what do they check?

In pairs for 5 minutes, then one example per table.

Ask the question once and stay quiet. The first answer is usually "anything with numbers", so push for the reason behind it.

Capture each table's example on the flip chart under two headings: never, and only with a human check.
