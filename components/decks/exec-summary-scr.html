Executive summary

Capacity, not demand, now limits our growth, so we recommend a EUR 85m hub-and-spoke build

Situation

Regional parcel demand is strong and we are well placed to serve it

  • Parcel volume has grown 14% a year since 2023 and reached 41 million in 2026
  • Revenue of EUR 310m at a stable 11% EBIT margin
  • Three depots handle 80% of all volume
Complication

Capacity and unit cost are now moving the wrong way

  • Depots run at 94% of peak capacity and overtime is up 31%
  • Cost per parcel rose 8% while average price rose only 3%
  • Two national rivals now offer next-day delivery in our home region
Resolution

Invest EUR 85m over three years in a hub-and-spoke network

  • Open two automated hubs to lift capacity by 60%
  • Refresh the six busiest depots and retire two weak routes
  • Reach a 14% EBIT margin and EUR 450m revenue by 2029
Source: Company finance ledger FY2026, network capacity study October 2026. Illustrative figures.2
