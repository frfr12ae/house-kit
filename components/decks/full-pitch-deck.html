Tallyhouse

Bookkeeping that closes itself.

Seed round, November 2026Maren Voss, co-founder and CEO

Introduce yourself in one sentence and the company in one sentence: Tallyhouse is bookkeeping software for small trades firms that closes the month without the owner doing it.

Tallyhouse is a fictional company and every figure in this deck is illustrative.

TallyhouseProblem

Every month, small trades firms lose a weekend to receipts.

  • 6.2 ha week on admin the owner never wanted
  • 41%file their VAT return late at least once a year
  • 1 in 3still keep receipts in a shoebox or a glovebox

Tallyhouse survey of 310 owner-managers of electrical, plumbing and heating firms, September 2026. Illustrative figures.

Make it concrete: the electrician who finishes a job at five and does paperwork until nine on Sunday. Do not read the three numbers, just point at them.

The point is that this is not a software gap, it is a time gap. The owner is the bookkeeper.

TallyhouseSolution

Tallyhouse reads every receipt, matches every payment and closes the month for you.

  1. 1CapturePhotograph or forward any receipt, invoice or bill.
  2. 2MatchEvery bank payment finds its receipt without a rule to write.
  3. 3CloseThe month is balanced, VAT is ready and the owner approves in one tap.

Three verbs, in this order. Capture is easy and everyone has it. Match is where our model earns its keep. Close is what the owner actually pays for.

TallyhouseWhy now

Three shifts make this possible now, and none of them existed three years ago.

  • 2027E-invoicing becomes mandatory for every business, so structured data finally arrives with each bill.
  • 94%of small business bank accounts can now be read through open banking, with the owner's consent.
  • <1cis the cost of reading one receipt with current document models, down from 12 cents.

An investor will ask why this was not built five years ago. Answer with the three shifts, in order of how hard they are to reverse: regulation, banking access, model cost.
