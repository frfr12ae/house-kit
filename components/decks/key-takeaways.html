Three takeaways

    Fewer projects finish faster

    Every extra project slows all the others, so cap the list.

  2. 2

    Every risk needs a name

    An owner and a first check turn a worry into work.

  3. 3

    Decide the stop rule early

    Agree what ends a project before it starts.
