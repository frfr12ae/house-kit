Network strategy review, FY26

Margin fell 2.9 points in a year, and three changes to the network recover almost all of it

Situation

  • Kestrel runs 10 lanes from 4 depots and moved 6.8 million parcels in FY26.
  • Revenue grew 9% to €212m.
  • Customers rate delivery reliability at 94%.

Complication

  • Operating margin fell from 8.4% to 5.5%, a drop in profit from €16.3m to €11.7m.
  • Empty running alone cost 2.0 points: one truck in three returns unloaded.
  • Three high-volume lanes earn below the network average.

Resolution

  • Redraw routes on three lanes, add a priced contract tier and sell return loads.
  • Together worth +2.8 points, back to 8.3% by Q4 2027.
  • Investment of €4.2m, paid back in 14 months.
Decision neededApprove the €4.2m programme and name a programme lead by 30 November.
Source: Kestrel finance ledger FY25 and FY26, route telematics, team analysis. Illustrative figures.2

Read the three columns left to right in under a minute. The numbers to remember: 8.4 to 5.5 percent, 2.0 points from empty running, 2.8 points recovered.

Land the decision here, then spend the rest of the hour showing why it is safe to say yes.

Three moves recover 2.8 of the 2.9 points lost, for a €4.2m investment

01

Redraw routes on the three lanes

Pair outbound and return legs, add one cross-dock and cut the longest runs by 40 minutes. Pilot on Harbour loop in Q1.

+1.3points of margin
€2.4m, owner: COO
02

Introduce a priced tier for tight delivery windows

Customers who need a two-hour window pay for it. Standard windows stay as they are, so no one is repriced without a choice.

+0.9points of margin
€0.6m, owner: Head of sales
03

Sell return loads to partner carriers

Offer spare capacity on return legs through a simple booking portal, starting with the four partners who already asked.

+0.6points of margin
€1.2m, owner: Network planning
Total+2.8 points takes margin from 5.5% to 8.3% by Q4 2027. €4.2m invested, paid back in 14 months.
Source: Kestrel finance model, October 2026. Impacts are run-rate by Q4 2027 and do not add the 0.1 point not recovered. Illustrative figures.7

Each move has one owner and one number. Together they recover 2.8 of the 2.9 points, so we are not promising a full return to 8.4.

If someone asks what happens if only one works: route redesign alone is still worth 1.3 points and pays back on its own.
