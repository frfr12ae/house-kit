Questions, answered

Cannot find what you need? Our finance specialists reply within one working day at help@stackwell.example.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their bank and ledger in under an hour. Matching rules are suggested from your last three months of history, so the first close already runs on a sensible baseline.

Which accounting systems do you connect to?

We read from the major cloud ledgers and from any bank that supports open banking. If your system exports files on a schedule, a secure folder drop works as a fallback.

Is our financial data used to train models?

No. Your data stays in your workspace, is encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never used to train shared models. Variance notes are generated per workspace and can be switched off.

Can auditors get read-only access?

Yes. Invite them with the auditor role and they see closed periods, supporting entries and the full change history, with no ability to edit anything.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing is charged automatically. You choose a plan when you are ready, and until then your workspace turns read-only so no data is lost.
