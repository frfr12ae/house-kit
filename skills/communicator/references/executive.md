# Executive mode

Purpose: help senior people make or endorse a decision quickly, on the right evidence. Covers proposals, decision papers, business cases, steering-group and sponsor updates.

## Structure (default, use loosely)

1. **Answer first:** the recommendation or conclusion in one or two sentences, with what you need from the reader ("Approve...", "Decide between A and B by 15 May").
2. **Why:** two to four reasons, each a full-sentence claim, not a topic label. They should not overlap and together should cover the case (MECE). Where causes genuinely overlap, say so rather than force them apart.
3. **Evidence:** the key numbers behind each reason, with source or assumption.
4. **Implications:** cost, time, risk, people, customers, regulation, as relevant.
5. **Risks and alternatives:** what could go wrong, what else was considered and why not.
6. **Next step:** owner, date, decision needed.

If the reader lacks context or may resist, open with one or two lines of situation and complication (SCQA), then the answer.

## Rules

- Lead with the decision, not the process. "I ran 12 interviews" comes later, if at all.
- Numbers with units and comparisons ("DKK 1.2m, 8% under budget"), not adjectives ("significant savings").
- Separate what the data shows, what you infer, and what you recommend.
- State confidence and the main uncertainty once, in one sentence.
- One page or less for the core message; detail in an appendix.
- No consulting jargon: leverage, synergies, holistic, move the needle, best-in-class.
- Bad news first too, with the plan.
- Status updates: what is done, what is blocked and by what, what is needed from the reader, when the next review is. Never "on track" when a dependency is blocked.

## When not to force the pyramid

- No recommendation yet: say so. "No recommendation yet. Here is what I know and what I will know by [date]."
- Brainstorming or option generation.
- Formats a client or regulator prescribes.

## Skeleton

**Recommendation:** ...
**Why:** 1) ... 2) ... 3) ...
**What it means:** ...
**Risks:** ...
**Decision needed:** ... by ... (owner)
